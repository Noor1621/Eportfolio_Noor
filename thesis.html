Master’s Thesis

One research story, two digital experiences.

Integration of SAR and Optical Satellite Data for Delineation of Dry and Wet Snow Cover in Hohe Tauern National Park, Austria.

Hohe Tauern · 2018–2025

Snow cover, climate and Earth observation.

The study combines Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical imagery, terrain, temperature and precipitation to investigate snow-cover dynamics. The workflow includes reproducible Python and GIS processing, classification, spatial analysis and visualization, with approximately 89% final accuracy.

Research focus

What the study investigates.

01 · Objective

To integrate Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical data using Principal Component Analysis for improved snow cover detection

02 · Objective

To classify dry vs. wet snow using Sentinel-2 and SAR backscatter

03 · Objective

To examine the influence of temperature and precipitation on dry and wet snow across elevation zones
