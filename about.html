About

A geospatial researcher with a visual mind.

My work combines spatial analysis, remote sensing, cartography, data visualization, UX thinking and digital communication.

“I enjoy the point where raw spatial evidence becomes something people can see, compare and understand.”

My background began in Geography and GIS and expanded through climate research, Earth observation and visual communication. I have worked across research, GIS analytics, planning, meteorological analysis and graphic design, building an interdisciplinary practice around spatial information.
